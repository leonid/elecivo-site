Electrical design for apartments, from floor plan to quotation

Draw the rooms, place sockets, switches and lights, and Elecivo groups them into circuits, sizes cables and breakers, checks the design and prices it. Every figure comes from one project, so the plan, the board, the bill of materials and the quote always agree.

One project, every output

  1. Plan

    Trace a measured drawing or draw the rooms to the millimetre; walls, doors and windows follow.

  2. Devices and circuits

    Place sockets, switches, lights and appliances. Elecivo suggests the circuits and routes the cables along the walls.

  3. Checks

    Cable capacity, voltage drop, breaker coordination, RCD loading, bathroom zones and start-up current, checked again on every change.

  4. Distribution board

    Rails, single-line diagram, wiring and labels, with a simulator to trip an RCD and see what goes dark.

  5. Materials and quotation

    Bill of materials, labour, supplier orders and a client quotation, with A3 drawings ready to print.

  6. On site

    Work log, crew hours and material on site, tracked against the plan.
